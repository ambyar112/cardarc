METHOD 3: Check Source Files DIRECT
1. Buka cardarc.vercel.app

3. Expand: cardarc.vercel.app → assets
4. Find: index-[hash].js (biggest JS file)
5. Ctrl+F search: "contract" atau "0x"
6. Look for addresses starting with 0x
7. Compare with expected addresses above
